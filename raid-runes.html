Three traps that quietly cost you slots

  1. Duplicates do nothing. Two copies of the same rune count once. Five copies still count once. Always use five different runes.
  2. Tribe-locked runes. Anything tagged HUMAN / ELF / UNDEAD / ORC only reaches part of your army — and for healing, nothing at all.
  3. Grade 6 isn't always an upgrade. Sometimes it changes the mechanic (stun resist becomes immunity — good), sometimes it just changes a displayed number (cleanse — no effect).
