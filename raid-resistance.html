Sand Resistance Damage Matrix

Sand resistance scales the damage you deal to each boss and the damage your corps take. Every encounter interpolates between its own floor and ceiling. Sweep across either heatmap to read exact multipliers — resolved every 10 resistance. The axis is logarithmic, because full-damage thresholds run 160, 320, 640 … 9,600 and a linear axis crushes every stage-1 row into the leftmost sliver.

Damage dealt

Your output multiplier, in eight bands so equal values line up across rows and can be counted off without a legend. Darker amber means you are under-resisted. Past ×1.00 there is nothing further to gain, so the row fills green — on a log axis that staircase of edges is close to a straight diagonal, one step per tier.

Damage taken

Incoming damage, diverging around ×1.00: rust is worse than baseline, blue is better. The eight mis-authored rows sit entirely below baseline, so they read blue on sight — no marker needed, though they stay tagged ↑ to tie them to the note.

×0.2×5+ midpoint = ×1.00 baseline

Eight of these fifteen rows have an inverted damage-taken curve

The game normalises the taken multiplier with t > 10 ? t / 100 : t, treating values above ten as percentages. Stage 1 rows are authored small — Dragon Knight is 6, so it stays ×6 and behaves correctly: more resistance, less damage taken. Stage 2 and 3 rows are authored large — Dragon Knight stage 2 is 20, which becomes 0.20 — so those curves run backwards, rising from ×0.20 toward ×1.00 as resistance climbs.

Affected rows are marked ↑. These are the values the engine actually computes, so they are correct for planning, but the underlying rows look mis-authored.
